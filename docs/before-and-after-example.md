# Before-and-after example

## Original draft
At the end of the day, it is important to note that artificial intelligence is being adopted by teams across many industries, and this is actually changing the way that people work. The tools are being used by developers to write code, by analysts to explore information, and by operators to automate repetitive tasks.

## Example feedback
- **Filler phrase:** “At the end of the day” delays the main point. Consider removing it.
- **Filler phrase:** “it is important to note that” announces importance instead of stating the point.
- **Possible passive voice:** “is being adopted” and “are being used” may hide the people choosing and using the tools.
- **Long sentence:** The opening sentence combines several ideas and can be split.

## Revised version
Teams across many industries are adopting artificial intelligence, changing how people work. Developers use these tools to write code, analysts use them to explore information, and operators use them to automate repetitive tasks.
